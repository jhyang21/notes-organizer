TidyNote

Talk it out. Get a clean note.

TidyNote takes a voice ramble — or a messy note you already have — and turns it into a structured note with a title, headings, bullets, and your action items. It organizes what you said; it never summarizes it away. Every fact, name, number, and date you spoke stays in.

How people use it

  • Record a thought while walking. Your iPhone transcribes it, TidyNote structures it, you save it to Apple Notes.
  • Share an existing note or any text into TidyNote from the share sheet and get it back organized.

What it costs

Tidies that run on your iPhone are free and unlimited. Premium tidies, which use a larger model on our servers, are capped at five a month on the free plan. TidyNote Pro makes them unlimited, with a 7-day free trial. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings on your iPhone, under your name → Subscriptions.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Tidies that run on your iPhone need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence; on other iPhones, premium tidies do the work.

Support

There is no account to recover and no password to reset. For anything else — a bug, a note that came out wrong, a question about your subscription — write to us. We read every message.
