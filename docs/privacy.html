TidyNote

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 August 2026

TidyNote has no accounts and no tracking. Every tidy runs on our server: the app sends your recording, or the text you shared in, over an encrypted connection, and gets the organized note back. We keep neither one.

TidyNote is made by immForm. This policy explains what the app handles and what leaves your phone. It covers the TidyNote iPhone app and its share extension.

We don't ask who you are

There is no sign-up, no login, no email address, no password. The app creates a random identifier on first launch — a string like tidy:1F3C… — and uses it to count how many tidies you have used this month and to check whether you have a subscription. It is not derived from your name, your email, your phone number, your device identifier, or an advertising identifier, and it is not linked to any of them. Delete the app and that identifier is gone.

What TidyNote sends

Every tidy runs on our server. When you record, the app sends the recording over an encrypted connection to TidyNote's server, which passes it to our AI provider to be turned into text and then organized, and returns the finished note to your phone. When you share text in instead, the same thing happens without the transcription step.

  • We don't keep your recording or your text. Each exists in memory for the length of the request and is not written to any database or log of ours.
  • Our AI provider is OpenAI. They transcribe the recording and organize the note. Under OpenAI's API terms, what we send through the API is not used to train their models, and it is retained for up to about 30 days for abuse monitoring before deletion. That covers recordings and the text made from them as well as text you share in. We name them here so you can judge that for yourself; the app itself does not display vendor names.
  • Our server keeps counters, not content. A row holding your anonymous identifier, the current month, and how many tidies it has used.
  • Rate limiting. To stop abuse, the server keeps a short-lived counter against a one-way SHA-256 hash of the requesting IP address. We do not store the address itself and cannot recover it from the hash.
  • Request logs. One line per request: how long it took, the response status, whether the plan was free or Pro, token counts, and a short hash prefix of the anonymous identifier. Your recording and your text never appear in a log.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your notes. TidyNote does not store your notes. Finished notes go where you send them — Apple Notes, the clipboard, the share sheet.
  • Diagnostics. The diagnostics log in the app stays on the device. Nothing is reported back to us.
  • Who you are. There is no account to make and no tracking of any kind, so nothing that identifies you is sent with a tidy.

Subscriptions

Apple processes every payment. We never see your card, your Apple Account, or your billing address. RevenueCat, our subscription provider, records whether the anonymous identifier above has an active subscription so the app and server know whether to allow unlimited tidies. That record is purchase status tied to a random string — not to you.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or sites.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.
  • No profile is built about you. There is nothing to build one from.

Who else touches the data

Three companies process data on our behalf, each for one narrow job: Supabase hosts the server and the counter database, OpenAI transcribes recordings and organizes notes, and RevenueCat records subscription status. Apple handles payment and app delivery under its own privacy policy.

How long anything is kept

Monthly quota counters are kept while they are in use and are not needed after the month ends. Rate-limit counters last minutes. Request logs are kept short-term for debugging. Your recordings and your text are not kept by us at all; OpenAI's abuse-monitoring copy of them is deleted on their schedule, about 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Share text in instead of recording. The text still goes to our server, but no recording of your voice does. The app asks once, before the first tidy leaves your phone, and you can decline — though nothing can be organized until you agree.
  • Delete everything on your side by deleting the app. That removes the recordings, the transcripts, the diagnostics log, and the anonymous identifier.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability. If you are in the EU, UK, California, or anywhere with similar rights, you may exercise them by writing to us. Be aware of the honest limit: the only data we hold is keyed to a random identifier we cannot connect to an email address, so we may not be able to find your records from a message alone. We will tell you what we can and cannot do.
  • Cancel a subscription in the App Store, under your Apple Account subscriptions.

Children

TidyNote is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Where data is handled

Our providers process data on servers in the United States. If you use the app from elsewhere, your recordings and your text are handled there.

Changes
