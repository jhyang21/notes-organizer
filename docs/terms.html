What the app does

TidyNote takes a voice recording or a piece of text and rewrites it as a structured note — a title, headings, bullets, and any action items you stated. It organizes; it does not summarize, and it is not meant to replace your notes app. Every tidy runs on our server: the app uploads your recording, or the text you shared in, over an encrypted connection and gets the finished note back.
